🧭 My Working Philosophy

flowchart LR
    A[Business Problem] --> B[Research & Evidence]
    B --> C[AI-Assisted Analysis]
    C --> D[Human Judgement]
    D --> E[Actionable Workflow]
    E --> F[Measure & Improve]

I am less interested in using AI simply to generate more output.

I am more interested in building systems where AI can help people:

research faster → structure information → detect patterns → challenge assumptions → make better decisions → execute consistently

Principles I try to build around

AI should improve decisions, not just generate content.

Evidence before assumptions.

Competitors are evidence, not blueprints.

Automation should remove repetitive work, not necessary judgement.

Useful AI workflows need guardrails, evaluation, and clear limitations.

Digital growth starts with understanding the buyer and the business problem.
